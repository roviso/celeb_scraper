Keyword | Early Life, Family (Siblings)

Born on July 3, 1962, in Syracuse, New York, Thomas Cruise Mapother IV had a challenging childhood. His father was an electrical engineer while his mother was a special education teacher. He was raised with his three sisters, moving frequently due to his father's job, including a stint in Canada. Cruise has admitted that his father was a bully who was physically abusive toward him and his siblings. During his school years, Cruise attended 15 schools in 14 years. A knee injury ended his interest in athletics, and he pivoted to acting during high school. 

Keywords | Education & Early Career

After high school, Tom Cruise moved to New York in hopes of starting an acting career. In 1981, he debuted with a small role in "Endless Love" and won critical acclaim for his performance in "Taps." Later, he signed with talent agency Creative Artists Agency (CAA) that led to his breakthrough role in "Risky Business." 

Keywords |Ethnicity, Nationality & Religion

Tom Cruise is of white ethnicity and is a national of the United States. In terms of religion, Cruise is a high-profile member of the Church of Scientology, known for openly advocating for the church and attributing it to his success in overcoming dyslexia.

Keywords | Quotes

Many of Tom Cruise's quotes reflect his personality and worldview, notably, "I don't believe in failure. It's not failure if you enjoyed the process", and "I've never done work for money ever. Keyword | Career

Tom Cruise’s acting career took off in 1983 with the release of "Risky Business." His blockbuster movie "Top Gun" in 1986 catapulted him to the status of an international superstar. He continued to earn acclaim and commercial success for roles in films like "Rain Man," "Born on the Fourth of July," "Jerry Maguire," and the "Mission: Impossible" series. He has also produced many of his films, amplifying his income and influence in the industry. 

Keyword | Dating History

Tom Cruise has been married three times: to Mimi Rogers from 1987 to 1990, Nicole Kidman from 1990 to 2001, and Katie Holmes from 2006 to 2012. He has three children from these marriages.
